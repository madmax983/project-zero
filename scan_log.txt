**2024-11-13 - [Security Scan Summary]
**Threat:** [Integer Overflow DoS]
- Discovered and patched vulnerable integer math operations capable of causing panics or OOB access `let idx = y * width + x;` in `resources.rs`, `dreams_of_genesis/mod.rs`, and `headless.rs`.
- Checked for memory safety and unsound `unsafe` code. Fixed `needless_borrow` in `ephemeral_moons.rs`.
- Scanned for dependency vulnerabilities with `cargo audit`. Found unmaintained `paste` and unsound `rand` versions, but they are transitive dependencies.
- Tested specific exploit test cases. All passed safely.
**Defense:** [Verified Safe] Safely refactored grid math to use `checked_mul` and `checked_add`. No action required for dependencies.
