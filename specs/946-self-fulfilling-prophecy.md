# 946: The Self-Fulfilling Prophecy

## 1. Overview

Watching harmless rumors snowball into a genuine colony-destroying crisis because people *believe* them. Rumors generated by the Chronicle system (e.g., a minor, handled food shortage) spread via Pop social interactions. Pops reacting to the rumor change their behavior, such as panic-hoarding food or abandoning posts to seek safety. Their actions create the very crisis the rumor described, which generates *more* rumors.
